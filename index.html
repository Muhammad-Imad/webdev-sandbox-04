Our story

little moments that now feel very special

Two separate worlds

Not long ago, we were two strangers living completely different lives. You were busy with lectures, wards, long study nights and a fluffy white cat waiting for you at home. I was somewhere else, living my own routine, quietly praying that Allah would send the right person into my life at the right time. Neither of us knew the other even existed.

A suggestion from Ainee

Then one day, your friend Ainee said something simple: "Why don't you try Muzz?" Maybe you laughed it off at first, maybe you thought about it for a few days. But you listened. Ainee probably had no idea that one small suggestion would end up starting such a beautiful story. Sometimes Allah sends His plans through the people closest to us.

Meeting on Muzz

Out of all the profiles in the world, we came across each other. What I liked from the very beginning was that we were both there for the same reason: not for time pass, but for marriage, with respect and honesty. There were no games and no pretending, just two people who knew what they wanted and were brave enough to look for it the right way.

The first few messages

I still remember how carefully I chose my words in those first messages. You don't want to say too much, you don't want to say too little. Every reply from you felt like a small green light. Slowly the formal "hi, how are you" turned into real questions: about family, about dreams, about what kind of life we each wanted to build.

Moving to Instagram

Then our conversation moved to Instagram, and it felt like a door opening a little wider. I got to see your world: your pictures, your smile, your cat, your little moments. We went from being a little shy to having long talks, and without even noticing, checking for your message became my favourite part of the day.

Getting to know the real you

The more we talked, the more I learned about you. A 4th year medical student who works so hard, tired after long days but still kind. Someone who wants to heal people. Someone whose heart melts for cats. Every little thing I found out made me respect you more, and made me want to know you even more.

Slowly, some chemistry

Chemistry may or may not be your favourite subject, but ours is slowly building. It didn't happen in one day, and I'm glad it didn't. It grew quietly, one conversation at a time, one laugh at a time. I really like this slow, steady pace. It feels real, and it feels right.

And next...

This story has only just begun. There are still so many pages left: meeting each other's families, understanding each other better, making duas for the same future. I don't know exactly how every page will look, but I know I'd love to write them with you. The rest of the pages, we'll write together, In shaa Allah.
